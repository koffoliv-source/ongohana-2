How it works

  1. Choose

    Individual sponsorship (one child) or collective (not tied to one child).

  2. Give monthly

    A fixed amount of your choice, for food, school and health.

  3. Follow and share

    Monthly newsletter, news, letters and birthday gifts.

Individual sponsorship

You support one particular child and build a bond with them. Everyone gets involved as they wish, within the rules that protect the children.

Collective sponsorship

Same principle, without being tied to one child: you support all the children and receive the news too.

Frequently asked questions

Can I meet my godchild?

Yes, if you can, come and meet him or her: many sponsors keep a very strong memory of it. Contact us to organize your visit.

Are the children protected?

Yes. Rules set in advance protect the children’s well-being. For example, you can send letters or a birthday gift within that framework.

How do I pay from France?

By transfer to Cyta Adga, our financial partner recognized as public-interest: your monthly gift is tax-deductible.

And from Benin or another country?

In Benin, an account is open in the name of the NGO. Elsewhere you choose the Benin or France account.
