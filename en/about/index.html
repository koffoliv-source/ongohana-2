A big family in Cotonou

OHANA means “family”. Our mission: to help orphaned, vulnerable and family-deprived children in Benin.

Our story

The idea of a non-profit to help children came from a Franco-Beninese team working in health and social care. Our priority: comprehensive care for orphans and abandoned children, especially the youngest (0-5 years). We believe the most important thing for a child is to be surrounded by a family, which is why we work to keep family ties and allow return to family.

OHANA is an independent organization with no ethnic, political or ideological character. Its name comes from a cartoon about an abandoned character who is taken in and loved despite their differences.

  1. 2016

    Our future president settles in Benin and gets passionate about child protection.

  2. 2017

    The project is born: OHANA is launched with a Beninese team.

  3. January 2018

    The center opens: the first child arrives and the NGO is officially created.

  4. School year 2018

    The children start school.

  5. January 2019

    The day care for neighborhood children opens.

  6. 2020

    A specialized educator joins the team and becomes the center’s director.

Our values

Protection

Every child’s safety and well-being come first.

Family

We protect family ties and support return to family.

Dignity

Education, care and respect for a more dignified life.

Transparency

Sponsors hear about life at the center every month.
