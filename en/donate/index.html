Frequently asked questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

If you live in France, you can transfer to our financial partner Cyta Adga, a public-interest association: your donation is then tax-deductible.

What is my donation used for?

It helps pay for the children’s food, schooling, health and activities, and the orphanage’s day-to-day running.

Can I give from Benin or another country?

Yes. In Benin, an account is open in the name of NGO OHANA. From another country you can choose the Benin or the France account: write to us and we will reply quickly.

Can I give by bank transfer or check?

Yes, contact us through the form and we will tell you how to proceed.
